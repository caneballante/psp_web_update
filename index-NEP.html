Additional observations

Patterns from the rest of the workbook reinforce the same story: respondents want the anniversary campaign to translate local credibility into national visibility and durable support.

Visibility is the campaign job

Responses repeatedly ask for greater public recognition, stronger national awareness, and clearer explanation of what NEPs do. The campaign can help local audiences see their NEP as part of a national program with shared credibility.

Funding and bipartisan support sit just below the surface

Many campaign hopes mention Congress, appropriations, reauthorization, funding stability, or bipartisan support. The anniversary is being treated as a storytelling moment with policy consequences.

ROI stories are concrete and compelling

Several responses include leverage figures or project-scale evidence: 19:1, 17:1, $6-7 per $1, $32M, $40 per $1, and a kelp restoration example growing from $20K to more than $12M.

Water quality remains the shared backbone

Top-priority answers often return to nutrient reduction, clean water, stormwater, wastewater, monitoring, hydrology, and habitat. Restoration is usually framed as both ecological recovery and community resilience.

Logo 2 has clear preference

Among logo responses, Logo 2 received 17 votes and Logo 3 received 2. Written feedback asks for readability improvements, fuller "National Estuary Program" language, and stronger ties to the Clean Water Act or national identity.
